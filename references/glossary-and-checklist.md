# Glossary and quality checklist

Cross-cutting quick reference for this skill: it does not develop any
framework in detail (that's covered in the other files under `references/`);
it only fixes terminology and serves as a final quality control before
delivering a report.

## Bilingual glossary (ES/EN)

| Acronym | Spanish | English | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| VUCA | Volátil, Incierto, Complejo, Ambiguo | Volatile, Uncertain, Complex, Ambiguous | Framework for describing an unstable, hard-to-predict external environment. |
| BANI | Frágil, Ansioso, No lineal, Incomprensible | Brittle, Anxious, Non-linear, Incomprehensible | Framework that replaces VUCA to describe internal/social disruption dynamics rather than just external volatility. |
| DAFO / FODA | Debilidades, Amenazas, Fortalezas, Oportunidades | SWOT — Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats | 2x2 analysis of internal factors (strengths/weaknesses) and external factors (opportunities/threats). |
| PESTEL / PEST | Político, Económico, Social, Tecnológico, Ecológico, Legal | Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, Legal | Analysis of the external macro-environment affecting a business or industry. |
| BMC | Modelo de Negocio Canvas | Business Model Canvas | Nine-block canvas for designing or validating how an organization creates, delivers, and captures value. |
| Ansoff | Matriz de Ansoff | Ansoff Matrix | Cross of product (existing/new) and market (existing/new) to map growth options: market penetration, product development, market development, diversification. |
| 7S | Las 7S de McKinsey | McKinsey 7S Framework | Organizational diagnostic framework covering 7 interdependent internal elements (strategy, structure, systems, shared values, skills, style, staff). |
| SMART | Específico, Medible, Alcanzable, Relevante, Temporal | Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound | Criteria for formulating well-defined, actionable goals. |
| OKR | Objetivos y Resultados Clave | Objectives and Key Results | System for cascading objectives over time through measurable, quarterly key results. |
| RACI | Responsable, Aprobador, Consultado, Informado | Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed | Matrix for assigning roles and responsibilities in a process or decision. |
| VRIO | Valor, Rareza, Imitabilidad, Organización | Value, Rarity, Imitability, Organization | Sequence of questions to assess whether an internal resource or capability is a source of sustainable competitive advantage. |
| BCG | Matriz BCG (Boston Consulting Group) | BCG Matrix / Growth-Share Matrix | Portfolio matrix that crosses market growth with relative market share (star/cash cow/question mark/dog). |
| ERRC | Eliminar, Reducir, Incrementar, Crear | Eliminate, Reduce, Raise, Create | The four-action framework of Blue Ocean Strategy for redesigning a value curve. |
| JTBD | Trabajos por Hacer | Jobs to be Done | Framework that defines competition and innovation opportunity by the "progress" the customer seeks, not by product category. |
| BSC | Cuadro de Mando Integral | Balanced Scorecard | Performance measurement system across 4 perspectives (financial, customer, internal processes, learning and growth). |
| CAC / LTV | Coste de Adquisición de Cliente / Valor de Vida del Cliente | Customer Acquisition Cost / Lifetime Value | Unit economics metrics: how much it costs to acquire a customer and how much net revenue they generate over the relationship. |
| NPV / VAN | Valor Actual Neto | Net Present Value | The value of a future cash flow discounted to today's terms, minus the initial investment. |

## Pre-delivery quality checklist

Before delivering any report from this skill, review:

- [ ] The analysis was explicitly labeled as Strategic or Tactical, with its time horizon.
- [ ] No more than 2-3 frameworks were applied unless the user expressly requested a full analysis.
- [ ] The prioritized recommendations have Impact and Effort assigned, not just an unordered list.
- [ ] The strategic narrative module was offered (not forced) at the end of the report.
- [ ] If the request was purely about brand/naming/brand positioning, it was redirected to `brand-strategy` or `brand-positioning` instead of forcing SWOT/PESTEL.
- [ ] The context questions (business, objective, horizon, scope) were asked only once, without repeating data the user had already given.

## Common anti-patterns

- **Applying SWOT as a mechanical checklist rather than as input to a real
  decision.** Filling in four quadrants without connecting them to a
  recommendation turns the framework into paperwork, not strategy.
- **Confusing "urgent" with "strategic."** Mixing up the time horizon without
  realizing it leads to treating a short-term tactical problem with the
  weight and formality of a long-term decision (or vice versa), and throws
  off the recommendations.
- **Applying McKinsey 7S without first having defined the strategy those 7S
  are meant to support.** The 7S framework diagnoses internal alignment
  *around* an already-chosen strategy; using it first leaves nothing to
  benchmark structure, systems, or culture against.
- **Offering a strategic narrative before there's a solid analysis behind
  it.** Telling a good story without a well-founded decision only produces a
  compelling narrative about a strategy that doesn't exist yet.
- **Treating VUCA and BANI as interchangeable.** VUCA looks outward (the
  market and environment are unpredictable); BANI looks inward (people and
  the organization feel fragility, anxiety, and a lack of meaning in the
  face of that same environment). Using one for the other blurs whether the
  problem is a market problem or an organizational one.
